# D-004 — PvP Scope

- **Status:** APPROVED (P0 scope)
- **Context:** Competitive play needs deterministic rules, authoritative validation and anti-cheat review that are not implemented.
- **Decision:** P0 includes bot-only play. Do not ship player-versus-player matchmaking or client-authoritative competitive outcomes in P0. Reconsider online PvP after deterministic replay, server authority, reconnect and anti-cheat review.
- **Approved by:** Project Owner (authorized by chat instruction; display name not provided)
- **Approved on:** 2026-11-10
- **Implications:** Bots and local simulations must not be presented as online PvP.
- **Rollback:** A later ADR may expand scope after technical and security gates pass.
- **Test fixtures:** Deterministic simulation tests and a future server-authority/anti-cheat review; online PvP is not implemented.
